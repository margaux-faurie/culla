# Code of conduct

This is a short adaptation of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Where this page is silent, the Covenant applies.

## The aim

Culla's issues, discussions and reviews should be a place where anyone can take part without harassment, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take criticism of the work, not of the person.
- Accept that scope is small on purpose and that some ideas will wait.
- Respect other people's privacy: do not post someone's photos, location or personal details.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, personal attacks, and sexual or demeaning language or imagery.
- Harassment in public or in private, including repeated contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, discussions, pull requests and advisories, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through GitHub private reporting: open the repository's Security tab and select "Report a vulnerability", and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are read by the maintainer, Margaux Faurie, and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does. The steps follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
